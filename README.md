## 🕯️ THE PLOT

> *Every sale leaves a trail. Most businesses never follow it.*

This project analyzes **online retail sales data** to uncover **revenue trends, customer purchasing behaviour and product performance**.

Using **Microsoft Power BI**, the raw sales data was turned into an **interactive business intelligence dashboard** that gives decision-makers the insights they need to act, not just a pile of numbers to stare at.

---

## 🔪 THE EVIDENCE *(What the Dashboard Found)*

| 🔍 Clue | 🩸 Finding |
|:--|:--|
| 🗄️ **Records investigated** | **10,200+** sales records |
| 📈 **Revenue** | **+15% YoY** |
| 💰 **Profit margin** | **+3.2 pp** |
| ⚰️ **Overstock** | **-7%** after product-level drill-through |
| ⏱️ **Manual reporting time** | **-35%** |
| 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Customer segments** | Lifetime value (LTV) tracked per segment |

---

## 🩸 WHAT THE EYE WATCHES

```
 🔴  REVENUE TRENDS       →  how sales rise, fall and repeat over time
 🔴  REGIONS              →  where the money comes from, and where it doesn't
 🔴  PRODUCT CATEGORIES   →  who is carrying the business, who is dragging it down
 🔴  PROFIT MARGIN        →  products that sell well but earn nothing
 🔴  CUSTOMER SEGMENTS    →  who buys, how often, and how much they are worth
 🔴  OVERSTOCK            →  inventory that sits and waits
```

## 🧟 THE HAUNTED INVENTORY *(Key Discovery)*

> Some products sit in the warehouse and never leave. They look harmless in a spreadsheet. On the dashboard, they glow red.

By adding a **drill-through page for product-level profitability**, slow-moving stock could finally be seen, which led to a **7% reduction in overstock**.
